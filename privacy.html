Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This policy describes how TomoBao Bot may handle information when you use its Discord commands and server features.

Before publishing: review the sections below against the bot's actual code, database, logs, and hosting setup. This is a starter policy, not a verified audit of data collection.

1. Information handled

Depending on which features are enabled, the bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, command inputs, and information needed to perform moderation actions. If moderation records or logs are enabled, these may include relevant actions and timestamps. Confirm which of these are actually stored before publishing.

2. How information is used

Information is used to respond to commands, enforce server permissions, support moderation and troubleshooting, and maintain bot functionality.

3. Storage and retention

Customize this section: identify whether data is stored in local files, a database, or a hosting provider; specify how long moderation records and operational logs are retained and when they are deleted. Do not claim automatic deletion unless implemented.

4. Sharing

Information may be visible to authorized server moderators when necessary for moderation or support. Discord processes information under its own privacy policies. Confirm whether any additional hosting, analytics, or other third-party services receive data.

5. Your choices and requests

You can stop interacting with the bot or ask your server administrator about server-specific moderation records. To request access, correction, or deletion of information controlled by the bot maintainer, contact TomoBao on GitHub. Requests may be subject to legal and technical limits. Confirm the contact channel and deletion procedure.

6. Security

Access to stored information should be limited to authorized operators. No system can guarantee absolute security. Never send passwords or bot tokens through Discord commands.

7. Updates

This policy may be updated when bot features or data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
